Comparison status

The 2025 draft, Collective Nudging that Scales. How many dogs do I need to herd sheep?, reports a NetLogo 7.0.3 study. HerdSim scaling_v2 is a separate Python simulation study. They share a broad question, a dog-count grid, a 10,000-tick deadline, and a 90% reliability threshold. They do not share the same task, arena, initialization, controller, or frontier definitions.

The draft was not rerun in HerdSim or NetLogo during the current program. Draft values below are transcribed from the draft PDF and its repository note. HerdSim values come from claim-stage outputs. The comparison is therefore an outcome contrast across protocols, not a replication test.

Here, "not reached" means the upper failure boundary was not found by D = 35. These are draft-reported values, not results regenerated by this repository.

The draft also reports a negative association between mean spread and success, including Spearman rho = -0.701 for S_bar against SR and rho = -0.828 for S_bar * N against SR over 110 condition aggregates. Its sensitivity and density-gradient analyses were not rerun here.

HerdSim outcomes used for comparison

For the strombom_multi baseline on compact starts, claim outputs report D_min = 2 for N in {5, 10} and D_min = 1 for N from 25 through 400. D_max = 35 is a tested-grid ceiling in every Phase 1 cell because no overcrowding pair was observed. It is not a measured upper collapse.

Changing the starting layout did not change baseline D_min at N in {50, 100, 200}, but it changed cost substantially. Kubo broadly matched the compact baseline, while Kubo on wide starts and FAT on larger flocks often did not reach R = 0.90 anywhere on the tested D grid. Those are HerdSim transfer results, not draft or NetLogo parity results.

Why the outcomes may differ

Plausible differences include the draft's hold and gate phases, scattered initial sheep, corner-starting dogs, containment geometry, and controller details. HerdSim uses a shorter open-interior drive with dogs already behind the flock. This is interpretation only. No controlled component-swap study isolated any one cause.

Consequently, "not reproduced" means that the outcome did not appear under HerdSim's different protocol. It does not establish that the draft result is erroneous.
