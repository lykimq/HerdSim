This compatibility page keeps the former research-plan path valid. The maintained plan, setup, methods, results, credibility notes, and generated data tables now live in their canonical locations.

Canonical documents

  • Main research plan: scientific questions, phases, dependencies, claim criteria, budgets, and decision gates.
  • Experiment setup and operator reference: frozen task, parameters, terminology, staging, and run guidance.
  • Controller method guides: Strombom, Kubo, and FAT implementation and interpretation.
  • Empirical summary report: completed Phase 1, 2, and 4 findings, claim snapshot, uncertainty, and limits.
  • Credibility and comparison: 2025 draft comparison, NetLogo scope, validation evidence, and claim boundaries.
  • Generated data appendices: run ledger and reproducible Phase 1, 2, and 4 tables with source paths.

Current status

Phases 1, 2, and 4 are complete at claim grade. Phase 3 and the long-horizon C2b check were skipped because the baseline produced no overcrowding cells. Phases 5 and 7 have not been run.

The machine-readable frozen defaults remain in ../../configs/canonical_grid.yaml. For a completed campaign, its copied protocol.yaml and provenance artifacts record the exact executed recipe.
