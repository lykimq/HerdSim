## What AI was used for

- Code: drafting, refactoring and bug fixes in the simulator and the analysis scripts, from a task the maintainer described.
- Tests: drafting tests and updating them when the source changed.
- Docs: drafting and editing the README, method docs and reports in `docs/`.
- Review: reading diffs for bugs, and checking numbers in the docs against the generated tables.

Research questions, experiment design and the reading of results are decided
by the maintainer. Every number in the results comes from running the code.
